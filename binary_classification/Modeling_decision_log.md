## 1. Complete Classification Modelling Decision Matrix

This decision log documents every design choice, architectural trade-off, and empirical justification made across all classification modelling stages (baseline establishment, imbalance treatment, algorithm selection, hyperparameter optimization, and benchmark synthesis).

| Step / Modelling Phase | Decision Area | Options Considered | Chosen Option | Reason (Why We Chose / Rejected) | Empirical Evidence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Problem Formulation** | Target Binary Definition & Clinical Focus | 1) 3-class prediction (`<30`, `>30`, `NO`). <br>2) Binary classification: Acute readmission within 30 days (`<30` = 1) vs. Non-readmitted / Late (`NO` + `>30` = 0). | **Binary (`<30` = 1, `NO` + `>30` = 0)** | *Rejected 1:* High clinical noise between `>30` and `NO` (post-discharge outpatient adherence and social determinant data do not exist), causing an accuracy paradox (~45%–50% balanced accuracy). <br>*Chose 2:* Directly targets the acute 30-day transitional care window aligned with CMS HRRP hospital penalty avoidance. | Positive class rate = 11.49% (train: 9,004 vs. 69,327). |
| **Cross-Validation Setup** | Validation Protocol & Fold Partitioning | 1) Single Train/Validation split (e.g., 80/20). <br>2) Standard K-Fold CV. <br>3) **Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation (`StratifiedKFold`)**. | **Stratified 5-Fold CV** *(with recognized patient-level grouping limitation)* | *Rejected 1 & 2:* High variance and class proportion drift across folds in an 11.49% minority setting. <br>*Chose 3:* Preserves the ~11.49% positive class proportion across all 5 folds. <br>*Methodological note:* Because `patient_nbr` was excluded from feature matrices to prevent identifier memorization, standard `StratifiedKFold` was applied without grouping. Encounters from the same patient may appear across different folds. Patient-grouped cross-validation (`GroupKFold`) is recommended for future evaluations. | 5-Fold Stratified split output across all model workflows (`StratifiedKFold(n_splits=5, shuffle=True, random_state=42)`). |
| **Evaluation Metrics** | Performance Criteria for Model Selection | 1) Raw Overall Accuracy only. <br>2) ROC-AUC only. <br>3) **Macro F1 + Macro Recall + Accuracy**. | **Macro F1 & Macro Recall (Primary)** | *Rejected 1 alone:* Severe class imbalance makes raw accuracy misleading; a naive majority-class classifier predicting 0 for all encounters achieves 88.51% accuracy while failing to detect any early readmissions. <br>*Chose 3:* Macro F1 and Macro Recall evaluate performance across both majority and minority classes, ensuring that high raw accuracy does not conceal poor minority-class sensitivity. | Baseline vs. Tuned metric logs across `models/*/results/`. |
| **Baseline Establishment** | Transparent Benchmark Architecture | 1) Complex deep learning ensemble. <br>2) **Linear Logistic Regression with SAGA ElasticNet**. | **Logistic Regression Baseline** | *Rejected 1:* Obscures whether nonlinear models add genuine predictive value over standard clinical linear models. <br>*Chose 2:* Clinical informatics standard; enables direct inspection of odds ratios and weights while establishing a transparent baseline. | Baseline metrics: Mean CV Macro F1 `0.5116`, CV Macro Recall `0.6085`, CV Accuracy `63.55%` (`models/logistic_regression/results/baseline_metrics.txt`). |
| **Algorithm Pruning** | Computational Feasibility & Tabular Suitability | 1) Include KNN and Kernel SVM. <br>2) Include Naive Bayes and Single Decision Tree. <br>3) **Prune unscalable/unsuitable models; focus on 6 core architectures**. | **Prune KNN, SVM, Naive Bayes, Decision Trees; retain 6 core candidates** | *Rejected KNN & SVM:* O(n²) to O(n³) complexity makes kernel matrix computation prohibitive on >60,000 training encounters per fold, with high prediction latency. <br>*Rejected Naive Bayes & Single Trees:* Independence assumption violated by correlated clinical features; single decision trees suffer from high variance on sparse categories. | Scoping audit: 78,331 training instances across 86 features; computational scaling analysis. |
| **Algorithm Selection Suite** | Candidate Model Architecture Pool | 1) Deep learning only. <br>2) Tree-based models only. <br>3) **Diverse 6-model suite across four foundational learning paradigms**. | **Diverse 6-Model Suite (LogReg, Random Forest, XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost, MLP)** | Benchmarks across linear, bagging, gradient boosting, and neural architectures on tabular clinical data: <br>1. Linear: Logistic Regression (SAGA ElasticNet). <br>2. Bagging: Random Forest (decorrelated bootstrap aggregating). <br>3. Boosting: XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost. <br>4. Neural: Multi-Layer Perceptron (feedforward). | Complete benchmarking suites across `binary_classification/models/`. |
| **Class Imbalance: Tree Models** | Cost-Sensitive Weighting in Tree Ensembles | 1) Downsample majority class. <br>2) Algorithmic cost-sensitive loss weighting (`class_weight='balanced'`). | **Algorithmic Cost-Sensitive Loss Weighting** | *Rejected 1:* Discards >50,000 viable patient encounters and critical clinical patterns. <br>*Chose 2:* Penalizes minority false negatives directly within split objective calculations without data discarding. | RF: `class_weight='balanced'`; CatBoost: `auto_class_weights='Balanced'`. |
| **Class Imbalance: XGBoost** | Native Gradient Splitting Weight | 1) Standard binary logloss. <br>2) Native `scale_pos_weight = 7.70`. | **Native `scale_pos_weight = 7.70`** | Explicitly reweights gradient and hessian steps inversely proportional to class frequencies (69,327 majority / 9,004 minority = 7.70). | XGBoost training and tuning workflows (`xgboost_workflow.ipynb`). |
| **Class Imbalance: Linear & Neural** | Resampling Strategy (Logistic Regression & MLP) | 1) Global SMOTE on full training set before CV. <br>2) Fold-isolated SMOTE inside `imblearn.pipeline.Pipeline`. | **Fold-Isolated SMOTE (`sampling_strategy=0.30`) inside pipeline** | *Rejected 1:* Causes synthetic data leakage from validation folds into training folds. <br>*Chose 2:* Guarantees synthetic samples are generated strictly from training folds. `sampling_strategy=0.30` synthesizes minority instances to reach 30% of the majority class count, leaving validation folds untouched. | Pipeline configuration in `logistic_regression_workflow.ipynb` and `mlp_workflow.ipynb`. |
| **Hyperparameter Optimization** | Optimization Framework & Search Strategy | 1) Manual Grid Search. <br>2) Random Search. <br>3) **Bayesian Tree-structured Parzen Estimator (Optuna TPE)**. | **Optuna Bayesian TPE Optimization** | *Rejected 1 & 2:* Grid search is computationally prohibitive over high-dimensional hyperparameter spaces; random search lacks directed convergence. <br>*Chose 3:* Directed probabilistic search over continuous log-scale regularization and discrete architecture parameters; 30 trials for RF, XGB, LGBM, CatBoost, LogReg; 20 trials for MLP. | Optuna study logs and parameter history in `models/*/results/`. |
| **Model Selection Decision** | Final Architecture Selection for Test Evaluation | 1) Select MLP based on highest CV accuracy (85.35%). <br>2) Select CatBoost based on highest CV recall (59.79%). <br>3) **Select Random Forest based on highest CV Macro F1 (0.5699)**. | **Random Forest (Tuned)** | *Rejected 1:* MLP's 85.35% accuracy is driven by majority-class bias (in-sample minority recall was only 19.0%; CV Macro F1: 0.5369). <br>*Rejected 2:* CatBoost had lower Macro F1 (0.5468) and lower accuracy (72.18%). <br>*Chose 3:* Random Forest achieved the highest **CV Macro F1 (0.5699)**, **0.5878 CV Macro Recall**, and balanced **78.98% CV Accuracy** under the primary balanced evaluation criterion. | Verified benchmarking table in `models/common/model_comparison.ipynb`. |
| **Model Serialization** | Artifact Preservation for Evaluation Handoff | 1) Re-train ad-hoc during evaluation. <br>2) Serialize best tuned estimator via `joblib`. | **Serialize tuned Random Forest to `random_forest_best_model.pkl`** | Guarantees strict evaluation hygiene: frozen candidate model artifact handed over to Member 4 for single-pass holdout test-set evaluation without re-tuning. | Saved artifact at `binary_classification/models/random_forest/random_forest_best_model.pkl`. |
